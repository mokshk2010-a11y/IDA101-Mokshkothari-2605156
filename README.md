# 🛡️ SignalSafe AI

> **An AI-powered digital fraud-safety agent built with Google Dialogflow ES**

SignalSafe AI is a conversational digital-safety agent designed to help users **identify potential scams, understand the level of risk, and take safer next steps**. It uses an intent-based conversational system to handle different real-world fraud situations and provide focused, easy-to-understand guidance.

---

## Project Overview

Digital scams often rely on urgency, impersonation, suspicious links, payment requests, or attempts to obtain sensitive information.

**SignalSafe AI** helps users navigate these situations through structured conversations rather than providing generic safety advice.

The agent focuses on:

* Recognizing scam warning signs
* Assessing suspicious situations
* Providing immediate safety guidance
* Supporting recovery after an incident
* Helping users preserve important evidence
* Improving long-term digital-fraud awareness

---

# 🚀 Main Features

### 🔍 60-Second Risk Scan

A quick conversational assessment that helps users identify important risk indicators and understand the situation.

### 🚨 Emergency & Recovery Guidance

Provides focused guidance for incidents such as:

* Money or payment compromise
* Account takeover
* OTP already shared
* Remote device access
* Threats and sextortion
* Suspicious transactions

### 💳 Payment & Banking Safety

Covers **UPI, QR payments, cards, ATMs, banking scams, refunds, KYC requests, and unusual payment requests**.

### 🔗 Suspicious Message & Link Analysis

Helps users reason through suspicious messages, links, websites, apps, and requests before taking action.

### 🎭 Impersonation & Deepfake Protection

Provides guidance for **fake identities, scam calls, voice impersonation, deepfakes, and impersonation warning signs**.

### 📱 Account & Identity Protection

Covers **password and email compromise, SIM swaps, account takeovers, identity-data exposure, and social/messaging account risks**.

### 🧪 Scenario Practice

Users can practice realistic scam situations, make a choice, and receive a debrief explaining whether the response was safe or risky.

### 📋 Evidence & Recovery Tools

Includes an **evidence-pack builder, urgent evidence checklist, recovery guidance, verified bank-contact guidance, and bank call-script support**.

# 🧠 Intents & Entities

SignalSafe AI contains:

| Component                         |  Quantity |
| --------------------------------- | --------: |
| **Intents**                       |    **54** |
| **Custom entities**               |   **100** |
| **Training/test user-says files** |    **53** |
| **User-says entries**             | **2,124** |

The intents cover areas such as **OTP and UPI fraud, phishing, account takeover, SIM swaps, investment and loan scams, romance scams, fake jobs, courier scams, shopping fraud, identity exposure, deepfakes, remote-access attacks, impersonation, emergency recovery, evidence collection, and scam-awareness practice**.

The **100 custom entities** allow the agent to recognize useful information such as payment methods, scam types, account types, communication channels, threat types, recovery steps, URLs, devices, documents, and other risk-related details.



. Conversation Intelligence

SignalSafe uses **Dialogflow ES intent matching, contexts, entities, parameters, follow-up conversations, and fallback handling** to maintain relevant conversations.
The agent is designed around a **triage-first approach**: understand the user's situation, identify important risk factors, then provide the most appropriate guidance.
It also supports **spell correction and natural variations in user messages**, allowing users to communicate in less formal, everyday language.
